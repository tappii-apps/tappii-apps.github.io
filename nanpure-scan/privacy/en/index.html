Number Place Scan Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 27, 2026
Last updated: October 31, 2026

This privacy policy describes how たっぴぃ (Tappii), the developer (the "Developer") of the Android app "Number Place Scan" (the "App"), handles user information in the App.

1. Images You Take or Select

The App uses images taken with the camera and images selected from the gallery only to read the digits of number place puzzles. All image processing is performed on your device, and images are never sent to or stored on external servers.

2. Saved Puzzles

The App stores the puzzles you save (the name, the digits of the puzzle, the digits you entered, your notes, whether you have solved the puzzle, and the date and time it was saved) on your device. Saved puzzles are never sent to external servers.

However, if you have turned on Android backup on your device, the Android backup feature may back up your saved puzzles to your Google account.

3. Information the App Collects

The App itself does not collect personal information such as your name, email address, or location. The App has no account registration and does not use any tools to analyze how the App is used.

4. Advertising

The App uses Google AdMob, an advertising service provided by Google LLC, to display ads on the solution screen. The App displays only ads that are not based on your interests (non-personalized ads).

Google AdMob may collect information such as your device's advertising ID, your IP address, and information about your device and how you view and interact with ads, in order to deliver ads, measure ad performance, and prevent fraud. For how this information is handled, please refer to Google's privacy policy.

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services

You can delete or reset your advertising ID in your device's Settings (the name and location of the setting vary by device).

5. Permissions the App Uses

  • Camera: used to take pictures of number place puzzles.
  • Internet access: used to display ads.

To select images from the gallery, the App uses the standard Android photo picker. The App does not request permission to access all photos on your device.

6. Sharing with Third Parties

Because the Developer does not collect personal information through the App, the Developer does not share any personal information with third parties. The collection of information by Google AdMob described in "4. Advertising" is subject to Google's privacy policy.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

The Developer may change this policy as necessary. Any changes take effect when they are posted on this page.
